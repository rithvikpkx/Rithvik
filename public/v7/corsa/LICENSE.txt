Changes: every badge, logo, wordmark, the seat-belt patch, caliper print, dashboard logo, wheel caps and the
licence plate were removed; all textures were dropped; the mesh was welded, simplified (827k -> 440k triangles),
given new creased normals, and meshopt-compressed. The stills are renders of this modified model.
